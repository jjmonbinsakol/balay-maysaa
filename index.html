Terms & Conditions

1. Booking & Reservations

All bookings are subject to availability and management approval. A booking is only confirmed upon receipt and verification of the required downpayment or full payment.

2. Payment Policy

A 50% non-refundable downpayment is required to secure your booking. Full payment must be settled on or before check-in. Walk-in guests must pay in full upon arrival.

3. Cancellation & Rebooking

Downpayments are strictly non-refundable. Rebooking is allowed with at least 48 hours advance notice, subject to availability. No-shows forfeit all payments made.

4. Check-in & Check-out

Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 NN. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

5. House Rules

  • No bringing of outside liquor inside the resort premises.
  • Quiet hours are strictly observed from 10:00 PM onwards.
  • No illegal activities of any kind are tolerated.
  • Guests are responsible for any damage to resort property during their stay.
  • Pets must be declared upon booking and are subject to a pet fee.
  • The resort is a private property — non-registered guests are not allowed without prior approval.

6. Guest Count & Pax Limits

The total number of guests must not exceed the capacity of the booked package. Additional guests beyond 24 pax are subject to an extra person fee of ₱500 per person.

7. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit may be collected upon check-in. This will be returned in full upon checkout, after inspection of the property, provided no damages are found.

8. Privacy

Your personal information is collected solely for the purpose of processing your reservation. We do not share your data with third parties.

9. Management Rights

Balay Maysa Resort management reserves the right to refuse or terminate any booking in case of violations of house rules or misconduct, without refund.

These terms may be updated by management at any time. By submitting a booking, you agree to the current version of these Terms & Conditions.
